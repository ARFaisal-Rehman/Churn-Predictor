Who should we contact?

Ranking by risk alone wastes budget on high-risk customers who spend little. Ranking by expected value (risk × typical spend) finds the peak sooner. The three inputs are assumptions; change them.

Expected profit of contacting customer i = riski × save rate × margin × typical 60-day spendi − cost. The save rate is a guess here; measuring it needs a controlled campaign.
